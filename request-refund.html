How to Request a Refund?

Use AI Support to get guidance about requesting a refund for an order.

1. Open AI Support

Open the AI Customer Support chat in the app.

2. Ask About a Refund

Describe what you need in natural language and provide your order number when available.

“I want to request a refund for my order.”

3. Provide the Order Details

The support assistant can use the order information available to your account to help identify the relevant order and explain the next steps.

4. Review the Refund Guidance

The assistant may provide information about the order, its current status, and the appropriate next step for requesting a refund.

5. Contact Support When Required

If a refund cannot be requested directly through the available flow, or if your request requires manual review, contact customer support.

Please note: Refund eligibility, refund amounts, and processing times may depend on the applicable merchant or service-provider policy. The AI Support Assistant provides support guidance and does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.
