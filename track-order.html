How to Track My Order?

Use AI Support to quickly check your order status and delivery information.

1. Open AI Support

Open the AI Customer Support chat in the app.

2. Ask About Your Order

You can ask a question in natural language or provide your order number.

“Where is my order?”

You can also ask questions such as “What is the status of order #12345?”

3. AI Checks Your Order

Your message is securely sent to the support backend. The AI identifies that you are asking about an order and retrieves the relevant order-status information from the order system.

4. View Your Order Status

The response may include:

  • Order number
  • Current order status
  • Payment status
  • Total amount and currency
  • Tracking number
  • Courier
  • Estimated delivery date

5. Understand the Order Status

Processing — Your order is being prepared.
Shipped — Your order has been shipped and tracking information may be available.
Delivered — Your order has been delivered.
Tip: Providing your order number can help the system identify the correct order more quickly.
Need more help?

If you cannot find your order or the information appears incorrect, contact support.
