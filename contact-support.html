How Can I Contact Support?

Get help when you need assistance with an order or the support service.

1. Open AI Support

Start by opening the AI Customer Support chat in the app.

2. Describe Your Issue

Explain your question or problem in natural language. You can ask about orders, delivery status, refunds, cancellations, or other supported topics.

“I cannot find the tracking information for my order.”

3. Include Your Order Number

If your request is related to an order, include the order number whenever possible. This helps support identify the relevant order.

4. Provide Relevant Details

  • Your account email address, when required
  • Order number, if applicable
  • A short description of the issue
  • Any relevant error message
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem

5. Request Additional Assistance

If the AI Support Assistant cannot resolve your issue, use the available support/contact option to request additional assistance from the support team.

Security reminder: Never share your password, OTP, PIN, authentication codes, or other sensitive security credentials with anyone claiming to provide support.
