How Do I Cancel My Order?

Use AI Support to get guidance about cancelling an order.

1. Open AI Support

Open the AI Customer Support chat in the app.

2. Ask to Cancel Your Order

Describe your request and provide your order number when available.

“I want to cancel my order.”

3. Provide Your Order Number

Including the order number helps the support assistant identify the order you are asking about.

4. Check the Order Status

The available cancellation options may depend on the current status of your order. For example, an order that is already shipped or delivered may not be eligible for cancellation.

5. Follow the Cancellation Guidance

The assistant will provide the available next steps based on the order information available to the system.

Please note: Cancellation availability can depend on the order status and the applicable merchant or service-provider policy. Submitting a request does not necessarily guarantee cancellation.
Unable to cancel your order?

Contact customer support if you need assistance with a cancellation request.
